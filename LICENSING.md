# Licensing: what you can use, and how

Short version: **the 3D models and the game kit code are free to use in your game, even one you sell.**
For voices, check your Fish Audio plan before you sell anything. Before you add anything from outside this repo, read
[Adding your own assets](#adding-your-own-assets).

> This page is a practical guide for hackathon teams, not legal advice. If you plan to sell your game,
> read the actual licenses (linked below) yourself.

## What CC0 means

CC0 means the creator gave up all their rights. The models are effectively **public domain**: you can use them,
change them, recolor them, sell games that include them and redistribute them, without asking anyone and without
crediting anyone.

Crediting the artists is still kind, and it costs one line in your credits:

```
3D models by Kay Lousberg (kaylousberg.com) and Quaternius (quaternius.com), CC0.
```

### Where each pack's license is recorded

- `game-kit/public/assets/<pack>/LICENSE.txt` names the license, author and **exact source commit** of every pack.
- [game-kit/docs/ASSET_LIST.md](game-kit/docs/ASSET_LIST.md) and the asset browser (`/assets.html`) show the license next to every model.
- Sources: KayKit packs come from the author's own [KayKit-Game-Assets](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets) repos.
  Quaternius packs come from community mirrors ([trebeljahr/quaternius-showcase](https://github.com/trebeljahr/quaternius-showcase),
  [J-Ponzo/gltf-universal-animation-library](https://github.com/J-Ponzo/gltf-universal-animation-library)) of
  Quaternius's free CC0 packs ([Quaternius FAQ](https://quaternius.com/faq.html): "can be used for free without the
  need for attribution in commercial, educational, and personal projects").

## Voices (Fish Audio)

- **Free vs paid.** Fish Audio's [terms](https://fish.audio/terms) limit free accounts to personal,
  non-commercial use. Commercial use is licensed for paid plans. A hackathon demo is fine. If you later sell the
  game or put it behind ads, the audio must come from a paid account. Terms change, so re-read them before you ship.
- **Cloning needs consent.** Only clone your own voice or the voice of someone who gave you written permission.
  Never clone a celebrity, a streamer or any voice you found online. See
  [fish-audio/docs/concepts/voices.md](fish-audio/docs/concepts/voices.md#licensing-and-consent).
- **Other people's public voice models** on fish.audio can be imitations of real people. To be safe, design your
  own NPC voices from a text description
  ([03-npc-voice-factory](fish-audio/examples/03-npc-voice-factory)) or use your team's own voices.

## Adding your own assets

Models, sounds, music, fonts and images you add are **your responsibility**. Before you drop a file into
`game-kit/public/`:

| The license says… | OK for a hackathon game? | OK to sell? | What you must do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CC0** / public domain | ✅ | ✅ | Nothing (credit is nice) |
| **CC-BY** | ✅ | ✅ | Credit the author in your game or its page, exactly as they ask |
| **CC-BY-SA** | ✅ | ✅ | Credit, and your changed version of *that asset* must use CC-BY-SA too |
| **CC-BY-NC** (NonCommercial) | ✅ | ❌ | Credit; never in anything sold, ad-supported or sponsored |
| "Free download" with no license, or ripped from another game, or "found on Google" | ❌ | ❌ | Don't use it |
| AI-generated by a service | Check that service's terms | Check that service's terms | Same as Fish Audio above |

Good CC0 sources: [kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl/assets) (models and sounds), [poly.pizza](https://poly.pizza)
(check each model, some are CC-BY), [quaternius.com](https://quaternius.com), [kaylousberg.itch.io](https://kaylousberg.itch.io).

**Keep a record.** Put a `LICENSE.txt` next to anything you add (e.g. `game-kit/public/sounds/LICENSE.txt`) with
the file names, author, license and source URL, the same way the kit does for its packs. Then credit everything
that isn't CC0 on your game's page or in a credits screen.

## Before you submit or publish

- [ ] Every file in `public/` that you added has a known license, recorded in a `LICENSE.txt`.
- [ ] Nothing is NonCommercial if the game will be sold or monetized.
- [ ] CC-BY assets are credited on the game page or in-game.
- [ ] Cloned voices are your own, or you have written permission.
- [ ] If the game is commercial: the voice audio was generated on a paid Fish Audio plan.
